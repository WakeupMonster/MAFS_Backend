MFS Edit Profile — Redesign Spec

Match@FirstWife · Current problems & proposed UI direction for Ojas

Current UI — 5 problems
1No progress indicator at top
User has zero motivation to complete their profile. No visual reward for filling fields. No percentage, no ring, no bar — nothing tells them "you're 72% done."
2Flat list with no visual hierarchy
Nickname, Gender, Height, Job Title, Company, School, Living In, About Me — all look identical. No sections, no grouping, no breathing room. It reads like a government form, not a dating app.
3Basics & Lifestyle sections are dense
8 items in Basics, 7 in Lifestyle — all stacked vertically with "Select >" labels. No description of why filling these matters. Users get bored and skip them entirely.
4No per-field percentage hints
User doesn't know which fields give the biggest impact. They can't prioritize what to fill first. A "+4%" nudge next to an empty field would drive action — right now there's nothing.
5Photos grid has no guidance
Just a 2×3 grid with a "+" button. No hint that more photos = higher %, no "profiles with 6 photos get the most matches" type encouragement.
